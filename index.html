About

Lyra is a voice-first personal AI assistant. Talk to her, ask anything, and get spoken answers in English, Telugu or Hindi.

What’s in v1.0.0

🎤 Voice assistant with “Hey Lyra” wake phrase
🔐 Voice lock – answers only your voice
💬 AI chat with saved history
🧩 Tools: camera, study, translator, memory, calculator, notes
👩 Girl voice picker and pitch control
🌐 3 languages · 🎨 colour themes

Privacy & Security

🔒 Your voice profile, notes, memory and chat history are saved only in this browser.
🎤 The microphone is used only while Lyra is listening.
📷 Camera photos are not saved or uploaded.
☁️ Your questions are sent to the AI service to get answers.
⚠️ Voice lock is a convenience filter, not a security lock.
